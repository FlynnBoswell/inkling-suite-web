## Word list (`static/words-en.txt`)

Inkling's spell-checker uses a word list generated by `scripts/build-wordlist.mjs` from the Hunspell `en_US` dictionary (Debian package `hunspell-en-us`), which is derived from **SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists)**, maintained by Kevin Atkinson. The generated list is lower-cased, expanded from affix rules and filtered. It is not otherwise changed.

`static/words-common.txt` (word commonness tiers used to rank suggestions) is generated by `scripts/build-common-words.mjs` from the SCOWL 2020.12.07 `english-words` size levels 10, 20 and 35, under the same terms below.
